ForecastGuard AI

AI-Based Forecast Bust Detection for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts

“AI-Based Forecast Bust Detection is an intelligent weather analytics system designed to identify situations where medium-range weather forecasts may significantly deviate from actual weather conditions. The system analyzes forecast errors, historical weather patterns and uncertainty indicators to estimate forecast bust risk and provide early warnings.”

The Problem

Medium-range weather forecasts (3 to 15 days) produced by numerical weather prediction (NWP) models frequently suffer from "busts"—sudden drops in forecast skill caused by chaos theory, mesoscale convective system trigger errors, and orographic boundary-layer misrepresentations.

Proposed Solution

ForecastGuard AI introduces an automated AI/ML supervisory layer that monitors operational numerical forecast tracks, cross-references historical ground-station errors, tracks ensemble member divergence (σ), and flags high-risk bust windows with probabilistic certainty.

Innovation Highlights

Unlike traditional weather apps that merely display deterministic NWP numbers, ForecastGuard AI evaluates forecast reliability. It quantifies uncertainty through ensemble spread analysis and provides explainable SHAP-style factor attributions.

Technology Stack

Frontend: HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript, Chart.js, Leaflet.js.
Backend: Python 3.14, FastAPI, Uvicorn, Pydantic.
AI/ML: Ensemble spread analysis, multi-parameter logistic risk regression, explainable attribution.
